Privacy

Privacy notice

How MetroMaya collects, uses and protects your personal information, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

MetroMaya is a prototype built as a course project. It is not an official government website, and no local government unit currently operates it.

What we collect

  • Your booking: business name and address, type of business, number of employees, the service, office, date and time you choose.
  • Your contact details: the owner’s or representative’s full name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your verification: a reference that tells us the same verified person made a booking, so we can limit each person to three active bookings.

Verifying with eGovPH

If you sign in with eGovPH, eGovPH sends us the profile details you agree to share. We keep only your eGovPH identifier, name, email address and mobile number. Your address is used once to fill in the form, and anything else eGovPH sends (such as ID numbers, photos or signatures) is discarded immediately and never stored or logged.

Scanning a government ID

If you upload a photo of your ID, our server reads the text on it and deletes the photo straight away. The text, never the photo, is then sent through AssemblyAI’s LLM Gateway to an AI model (Anthropic’s Claude) that picks out your name, birth date, ID number, expiry date and address. We keep your name and a one-way scrambled reference made from your details, not your ID number itself. Expired IDs and documents that are not government IDs are rejected.

Your location on the Office step

If you press Use my location, your browser shares your position with this page only. To draw the route, the position is sent to the OpenStreetMap routing service (OSRM). If you type a starting place instead, that text is looked up with OpenStreetMap’s geocoder. If you open Google Maps, your position goes to Google. MetroMaya’s server never receives or stores your location.

Saved on your device

While you fill in the booking form, your answers (not your verified name or verification) are saved in your own browser so they come back if you close the page. They are deleted when you finish booking, press Start over, or after 14 days. Bookings you make or open on Track a booking are also saved in your browser, without the private access code, so the details and check-in QR open with a weak signal. Remove them from that page at any time, or clear this site’s data in your browser. None of this is sent to our server.

Waitlist for fully booked days

If you ask to be told when a place opens on a fully booked day, we store your email address with that office and date, send you one email when a place opens, and use it for nothing else.

Why we use it

To check that a real person is booking, to keep out spam and duplicate bookings, to book and manage your appointment, and to show you the fees, inspections and documents for your visit.

Where it is kept

Bookings are stored in a database (Supabase) that only MetroMaya’s server can read or change. To view or manage a booking, you need both its reference number and the private access code issued when you booked. The server stores a hash of that code for access checks. When email reminders are enabled, it also stores an encrypted copy so it can send your private link. The office check-in QR contains a separate lookup code that works only after authorized staff sign in. Staff check-ins, queue numbers, no-shows, and treasurer-verified city hall receipt records are stored with the booking. If email is configured, your address and booking details are sent to the email provider for confirmations and reminders.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to have your data erased or blocked, to data portability, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. You can cancel a booking at any time on Track a booking.

What is MetroMaya

One trip to city hall, not three

MetroMaya puts the slow parts of a business permit online. You know the fees, the documents and your time slot before you leave, and Maya keeps you company along the way.

No surprises at the counter

Pick your city, type of business and number of employees, and MetroMaya shows the fees, inspections and documents before you book.

Skip the queue

Book a weekday slot up to 30 days ahead and walk in at your time. Every slot shows how many places are left, and no account is needed.

Stay in control

Your reference number lets you look up, move or cancel the booking yourself. No calls, no forms.

How It Works

Four steps to a confirmed slot

Booking takes about three minutes. You get a reference number straight away.

  1. Choose a service

    New permit, annual renewal, or an inspection visit.

  2. Describe your business

    Your type of business and number of employees set your fees and documents.

  3. Pick an office, date and time

    Weekdays, up to 30 days ahead, at a desk that serves your business.

  4. Bring your documents

    Your confirmation lists exactly what to bring and what you will pay.

Book a slot

Takes about three minutes. You get a reference number at the end.

    First, verify it’s you

    To keep out bots and spam, every booking starts with a quick identity check. Choose one.

    Tick the box above to choose a way to verify.

    Verify with eGovPH

    Sign in with your eGovPH account using a one-time code and your eGov PIN. Your name, email and mobile number are filled in for you.

    Scan your ID

    Take or upload a clear photo of the front of a valid government ID, such as a driver’s license, PhilSys national ID, passport or UMID. Your name is read from it.

    We read the text on your ID on our server, then an AI model (through AssemblyAI) picks out your details from that text. The photo is deleted right away and never stored. How we handle it

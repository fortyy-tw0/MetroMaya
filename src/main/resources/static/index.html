      Booking takes about three minutes. You get a reference number straight away to change or cancel later.

      1. Choose a service

      3. Pick an office, date and time

        Weekdays, up to 30 days ahead. Every slot shows how many places are left.

      4. Bring your documents

        Your confirmation lists exactly what to bring and what you will pay.

      What you pay depends on what you run

      Your type of business sets the base fee and the inspections. Your size then scales the fee and can add more checks.
